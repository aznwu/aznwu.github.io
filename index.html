Newsbot

A personal tool that turns each day's top news into one short video and posts it to my own social media accounts.

What it does

Once a day, on my own computer, Newsbot:

  • reads the latest headlines from public news feeds (RSS) of major outlets;
  • picks the day's most important stories and writes a short, neutral summary of each, using Anthropic's Claude;
  • turns them into a vertical slideshow video;
  • posts that video to my own accounts on X, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads and YouTube.

Who uses it

Only me. Newsbot is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and nobody else signs in to it. It only ever acts on accounts I own.

YouTube

Newsbot uses YouTube API Services to upload the daily video to my YouTube channel as a Short. By watching those videos you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Newsbot handles data is described in the privacy policy.
