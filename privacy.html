Privacy policy

For Newsbot, a personal tool that posts a daily news-summary video to its owner's own accounts. Effective September 28, 2026.

Who this covers

Newsbot is operated by Aleksandar Zivkovic for his own use. The only person who signs in to it, and the only account holder whose data it touches, is its operator. It has no other users and collects nothing from visitors to this website or from people who watch the videos.

YouTube API Services

Newsbot uses YouTube API Services. By using Newsbot you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Newsbot accesses in your Google account

When the operator signs in, Newsbot asks for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (upload), to upload the daily video to the operator's own channel, with its title, description and settings.
  • View your YouTube account (read-only), to read the channel's name and ID and the titles of its recent uploads. This is used only to confirm the right channel and to avoid uploading the same day's video twice.

Newsbot does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, or anything else in the account, and it does not access any other Google service.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token that lets Newsbot upload without asking every day. It is stored only in a file on the operator's own computer.
  • A record of each upload (video ID, link, title and date), kept on the same computer for 30 days and then deleted automatically, together with the daily log files.

Nothing is stored on any server run by Newsbot, because there isn't one.

Sharing

Information from Google or YouTube is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising. It is not sent to Anthropic or to any other service. The only thing Anthropic's Claude receives is public news headlines and snippets, which it uses to pick and summarise the day's stories.

Newsbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page for third-party access: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once revoked, the stored sign-in token stops working and is deleted from the operator's computer, and upload records are removed within 30 days. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email the address below.

Cookies and this website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. It loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
